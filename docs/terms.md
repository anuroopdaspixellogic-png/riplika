## 2. What the app does

Riplika pairs you with one random, anonymous stranger for a live video and text
chat. There is no account, no history and no way to find a stranger again. You
can end a chat at any time with **Next** or **Stop**.

## 4. Reporting and blocking

Every chat has a **Report** button. A report sends the recent chat context and
your reason to our moderation team, who review reports and can block users
permanently. Use **Block** to make sure you are never paired with that person
again.

We may remove content, suspend accounts or report users to law enforcement when
we believe these rules have been broken. We will act on valid legal requests
from law enforcement.
