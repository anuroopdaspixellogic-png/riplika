## 1. Who we are

Riplika ("we", "us", "the app") is an anonymous random video chat application.
The app is operated by [operator name / company], reachable at
[support email address].

## 2. What the app is

Riplika connects you with one random stranger at a time for a live video and
text chat. The app does not ask for an account, a name, an email address or a
phone number. A chat ends when either person presses **Next** or **Stop**.

## 3. What we collect

| Data | Why | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camera and microphone streams | To show your live video and play your voice during a chat | Streamed live to the other person; never recorded or stored by us |
| Chat text messages | To deliver them to the person you are chatting with | Held in memory while the chat is active; not stored after the chat ends |
| Reports you submit | To review abuse and act on it | Stored in our database, deleted after `REPORT_RETENTION_DAYS` (default 90 days) after review |
| Blocks you create | To stop the app pairing you with that person again | Stored so the block keeps working |
| A random anonymous identifier | To link your blocks and reports to you without an account | Stored on your device and in our database; clearing app data creates a new identity |
| Your IP address | Rate limiting, abuse prevention and keeping the service available | Kept in server logs for a short period, then discarded |

We do **not** collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos
from your gallery, location, or advertising identifiers.

## 4. Who your chat partner can see

Media and messages are sent directly between you and the person you matched
with, or through a relay (TURN) server when a direct connection is not
possible. Your chat partner can record you or your conversation. Do not share
anything you would not want a stranger to keep. If you believe someone recorded
you, report them and block them immediately.

## 5. Third parties

- **TURN/STUN servers** relay media traffic when a direct connection cannot be
  established. Those providers can see your IP address.
- **Our hosting provider** stores the server data described above on our behalf.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs or data brokers.

## 6. Legal bases and your rights

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we process data to provide the service
you requested, to prevent abuse, and to comply with legal obligations. You have
the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to
processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Contact
[support email address] and we will respond within [number] days. Because the
app is anonymous, we can only act on requests from the anonymous identifier
that created the data.

## 7. Children

Riplika is intended for adults aged 18 and over. The age confirmation in the app
is self-attested and we cannot verify it. We do not knowingly allow children to
use the app. If you believe a minor is using the app, contact us.

## 8. Security

Traffic is encrypted with TLS, access to the moderation dashboard requires a
secret token, and we apply rate limits to reduce abuse. No system is perfectly
secure, so please do not rely on the app to keep you safe from a determined
stranger.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. If we make a material change we will update the date
above and, where appropriate, notify users in the app.
